Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026 · Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run any servers of our own. Your game progress never leaves your device. The only parties that receive any data are Apple, when you make a purchase or use Game Center, and Google AdMob, if you choose to watch a rewarded ad.

1. Who we are

Nezha and Wukong ("the app", "the game") is an independently developed iOS game. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the app's developer. You can reach us at sychenshangyu@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

None. The app does not require registration, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and does not transmit any data to servers operated by us. We operate no backend and hold no database of players.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in the app's own private storage on your iPhone:

  • Game progress: campaign stage, tutorial completion, best Endless wave, win/loss counts per hero
  • Entitlements: which heroes and passes you have unlocked
  • Energy state: current Energy, the timestamp it last regenerated, and how many rewarded ads you have watched today
  • Your chosen interface language

This data stays on your device. It is not readable by us or by other apps. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

4. Information handled by Apple

In-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. We receive only a confirmation from Apple that a given purchase succeeded on the device.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your scores (best Endless wave, total wins, per-hero win rate) to Apple's leaderboards so you can be ranked against other players. Game Center displays the nickname you chose in your Apple Account settings. This service is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the iOS Settings app, and the game will continue to work without leaderboards.

5. Advertising

The app includes optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown automatically — an ad plays only when you deliberately choose to watch one in exchange for Energy. If you never tap that button, no ad is ever requested and no ad-related data is ever sent.

When you do watch an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect technical information such as your device model, operating system version, coarse region, and a resettable advertising identifier, in order to deliver and measure the ad. This processing is carried out by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms.

The app does not display the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. As a result, ads served are non-personalized. You can further limit ad tracking on your device under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

6. Analytics

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, or telemetry SDK of any kind. We do not know how many matches you play, which heroes you use, or whether you opened the app at all.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for a parent to request deletion of on our side. If you have concerns, please contact us.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete all your data: delete the app from your device. All locally stored progress is erased.
  • Remove leaderboard entries: manage or reset these in the iOS Game Center settings, or contact Apple.
  • Limit ad data: simply do not watch rewarded ads, or use the iOS tracking settings described in section 5.
  • Access, correction, or complaint: because we hold no personal data about you, we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete records on request — there are none. You may still contact us with any privacy question.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the CCPA, or similar laws. Those rights apply to personal data a company holds; as described above, we hold none.

9. Data we would never do

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not link any data to your identity.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects data handling in the app, note it in the app's release notes.
