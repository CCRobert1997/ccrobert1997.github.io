Privacy Policy

Roaring Alpaca · Last updated 27 August 2026

Roaring Alpaca is a single-player and local-multiplayer game. We designed it to collect as little as possible. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

1. The microphone and speech recognition

The game is controlled by your voice, so it uses your device's microphone while you hold the shout button. Your speech is converted to text using Apple's on-device speech recognition to figure out which phrase you shouted. Your audio and its transcript are used only, in the moment, to trigger attacks in the game. We do not record, save, or transmit your audio or transcripts. When you are not holding the button, the microphone is not listening.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account. There is no sign-up and no login in the app.

3. Game progress

Your progress — learned phrases, current level, and your character's appearance — is stored locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (the Premium Phrase Pack) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Whether you own the pack is determined through Apple's StoreKit on your device.

5. Analytics and advertising

The app contains no third-party advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

6. Software updates

The app may download over-the-air content updates to deliver improvements without a full App Store update. These updates are delivered as app content and are not used to collect personal information about you.

7. Children

Roaring Alpaca is rated 16+ and is intended for older teens and adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the CCPA, or similar laws. Those rights apply to personal data a company holds; as described above, we hold none.

9. Data we would never do

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not link any data to your identity.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects data handling in the app, note it in the app's release notes.
