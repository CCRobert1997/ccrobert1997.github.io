Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Hold the big button and yell into your microphone. The game turns your shout into an attack on the boss — the louder and more fired-up you are, the harder you hit. Chain different phrases for combos. You can play through the Campaign, go wild in Endless, battle the AI in Versus, or pass one phone to a friend for a 2-Player duel.

Why does the app need my microphone?

The whole game is controlled by your voice, so it needs the microphone to hear you. Speech is recognized on your device to detect which phrase you shouted. Your audio is not recorded, stored, or uploaded anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The mic doesn't seem to hear me. What can I do?

Make sure you granted microphone and speech-recognition permission when asked (you can re-enable them in Settings › Roaring Alpaca). Speak up — the game rewards a real, loud shout over quiet talking. A quieter room helps too.

I paid for the Premium Phrase Pack, but it's gone. How do I get it back?

Open the in-game Skills screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. The pack is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it carries over to any device you sign in on, at no extra cost.

What does the Premium Phrase Pack include?

It's a single one-time purchase (no subscription) that permanently unlocks all of the advanced phrases and their special attacks. Everything you unlock stays unlocked forever.

Is this app suitable for kids?

No. Roaring Alpaca contains crude humor and mature language as part of teaching authentic, colloquial Chinese expressions, and it is rated 16+. It's intended for older teens and adult learners and language enthusiasts.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress (learned phrases, level, and character look) is stored locally on your device. Your Premium Phrase Pack purchase is tied to your Apple Account and restores on any device via Restore Purchases.
